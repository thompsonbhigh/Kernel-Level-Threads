Scheduler()
    - ready queue of thread objects
    - may want pointer to currently running thread
    - may want end of quantum handler in round-robin implementation

Scheduler yield()
    - can only be called by currently running thread
    - gives up CPU, after figuring out who is next thread, then remove new thread from ready queue and dispatch to it
    - put self at end of ready queue, look at who is at front of ready queue, remove that thread from ready queue, and dispatch to it
    - good idea to have idle thread so that yielding thread does not context switch to itself
    - idle thread only calls yield()

Scheduler resume()
    - thread has been blocked and wants to resume execution
    - just puts on ready queue

end of quantum handler
    - at end of quantum, resume() current thread to add to ready queue, then yield()

Scheduler terminate()
    - check if thread is running before removing it from ready queue and allowing it to be terminated
    - give control to next thread, then destroy thread
